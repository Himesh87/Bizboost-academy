Privacy

This version of BizBoost Academy is a browser-based academic demonstration. Profile details, course progress and simulator results are stored in your browser using localStorage. They are not sent to a BizBoost server.

What is stored?

The demo may store the profile information you enter, course progress, task state and your latest simulator plan. Avoid entering sensitive or confidential information.

Clearing your data

You can clear the demo data by clearing this site's browser storage. Because there is no server database in this version, clearing the browser data removes the local records.

Contact form

The contact form demonstrates validation and local feedback only. It does not send an email.
